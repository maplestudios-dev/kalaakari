# 01 · Product Vision

## What KALAAKAARI is
A Delhi-born, independent creative studio website + admin CMS. The site is positioned as a branding-first studio for ambitious brands — not a faceless agency template, not a SaaS landing page.

The product is two surfaces:
1. **Public website** — homepage, about, services, work, contact (with case studies, blog and careers framed in the same system).
2. **Admin dashboard** — JWT-authed portfolio CMS + contact lead inbox + analytics.

## Target users
**Public site visitors**
- Founders building D2C, F&B, fashion, beauty, jewellery, hospitality, FMCG, consumer-tech brands
- Marketing heads buying brand + campaign + content + digital as a system, not in silos
- Press and prospective hires looking for the studio's POV

**Admin users**
- Studio owner / partner
- Producer or content lead managing portfolio + leads

## Primary business goals
1. **Generate qualified inbound briefs.** Contact form is the conversion mechanism.
2. **Make the studio's POV legible.** The homepage is a manifesto, not a brochure.
3. **Showcase work with editorial confidence.** Each project earns its own page.
4. **Make content updatable in-house.** Non-developers should manage portfolio and leads.

## What makes this different
- Editorial, cinematic, Indian-without-cliché design language
- English × Devanagari typography as identity, not decoration
- Senior-led brand voice (no Lorem-ipsum hedging)
- Motion subordinated to typography
- CMS designed around the actual content this studio ships
